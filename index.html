WHY THIS MATTERS

Online consensus is controlled by platforms.

Social metrics measure visibility and popularity. They don't necessarily measure independent collective intent.

◎

↗

ANTI-COLLUSION MECHANISM

Why commit-reveal?

The vote is hidden during the commitment phase, preventing participants from simply following visible early results.

01

Commit Phase

Your vote is hidden behind a cryptographic commitment.

vote + secret → hash
→
02

Reveal Phase

You reveal the original vote and secret after commitments close.

vote + secret → reveal
→
03

Verification

The system recalculates the hash and checks that it matches.

hash === commitment ✓
